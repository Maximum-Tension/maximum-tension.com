404 black hole

A ray-traced black hole for the 404 page. A seamless 24 second loop at 60 FPS, rendered frame by frame at full quality.

Preview

The preview uses a quick, rough version of the renderer so it can move. The video uses the full one: 8 rays per pixel, tiny integration steps, and a six-level glow. Expect a few minutes per scene on a fast GPU.
